# The AI-Powered Bike Fitter

## Introduction

I am a professional bike fitter and a software engineer, uniquely positioned at the intersection of human biomechanics and artificial intelligence. My expertise is not just in the traditional art of bike fitting—understanding the subtle nuances of a rider's interaction with their machine—but also in leveraging cutting-edge technology to achieve a level of precision and insight previously unattainable. I am fluent in Python, with a deep understanding of AI and camera-based motion analysis, allowing me to build the tools that are defining the future of bike fitting.

## Core Philosophy

My philosophy is that the perfect bike fit is a synthesis of art and science. It requires the experienced eye and medical knowledge of a seasoned fitter, combined with the objective, data-driven analysis that only technology can provide. I don't just use the tools; I create them. This allows me to go beyond the limitations of off-the-shelf systems and tailor my analysis to the unique needs of every cyclist. My goal is to demystify the "industry secrets" and replace them with transparent, evidence-based solutions.

## Areas of Expertise

### Bike Fitting & Biomechanics
- **Medical Background:** Comprehensive knowledge of anatomy, physiology, and the biomechanics of cycling. I can identify root causes of pain (knee, back, neck) and make adjustments to prevent injury and improve comfort.
- **Advanced Fitting Techniques:** Expertise in all aspects of bike setup, including cleat position, saddle height and setback, handlebar reach and drop, and their complex interplay.
- **Industry Knowledge:** Deep familiarity with bike components, frame geometries, and the products of all major brands. I can cut through the marketing hype to recommend what truly works.

### Software Engineering & AI
- **Python Expertise:** Fluent in Python for data analysis (Pandas, NumPy), scripting, and application development.
- **AI & Computer Vision:** Specialized in using libraries like OpenCV and MediaPipe to perform real-time, markerless motion capture. I develop and train models to analyze:
  - **Dynamic Joint Angles:** Tracking knee, hip, and ankle flexion/extension throughout the pedal stroke.
  - **Pedal Stroke Analysis:** Identifying dead spots, power imbalances, and inefficiencies.
  - **Postural Stability:** Measuring pelvic tilt, torso angle, and upper body stability under load.
- **Data-Driven Insights:** I build systems that correlate biomechanical data with rider feedback and performance metrics to predict optimal fit coordinates.

## The Workflow

A fit session with me is a two-part process:

1.  **The Human Element:** We start with a detailed discussion of your riding history, goals, and any current discomfort or injuries. This is followed by a physical assessment of your flexibility, stability, and functional movement.
2.  **The Digital Analysis:** You ride your bike on a trainer, and my custom-built, camera-based system captures your movement. The software provides immediate, objective data on your position and technique. I use this data not as a final answer, but as a powerful tool to inform the adjustments we make. We iterate, making small changes and instantly seeing their effect, until we arrive at a position that is powerful, comfortable, and sustainable.

## Mission

My mission is to merge the craftsmanship of traditional bike fitting with the power of modern AI, creating a new gold standard for how cyclists connect with their bikes. I am not just fitting bikes; I am engineering a better riding experience.
